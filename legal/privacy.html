Privacy

This page is a plain-language starter policy. Before going live, it needs Kareem’s legal business details, the selected booking and payment providers, and review for the countries where services are offered.

Information collected

When you use the website or book a session, the information collected may include your name, email address, appointment preferences, booking notes you choose to provide, and technical information such as referral source or browser data. Please do not submit sensitive health or medical information through a booking form.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, arrange and deliver coaching sessions, send booking-related messages, process payments through the chosen provider, and understand how visitors find the website. It is not sold as a standalone marketing list.

Booking and payments

Availability, appointment confirmations, and payment processing are handled by third-party services selected for the booking flow. Their privacy policies apply to information processed in their systems. The website is designed so payment card details are entered directly with the payment provider, not stored on this site.

Website analytics

With your permission, this site uses Google Analytics to understand visits, traffic sources, page use, and interactions such as booking-link clicks. Analytics does not load until you choose “Allow analytics.” You can change that choice at any time using the “Privacy choices” button shown on the site.

Google may receive technical information such as page address, referral source, campaign parameters, browser and device information, and interaction events. Advertising personalization is disabled in this website’s Analytics configuration. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, contact hello@kareemrizik.com. This contact address and the final policy details must be verified before launch.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the website, services, or providers change. The live version should show an effective date and the legal entity responsible for processing personal information.
