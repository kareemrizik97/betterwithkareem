One to one

Coaching session

One-to-one coaching to help you understand what's going on, work through what's keeping you stuck, and take meaningful steps toward what you want.

Your pace

This isn't about me telling you how to live. We work with your situation, your values, and the direction you want to take.

The work

Make sense of what is keeping you stuck.

Coaching gives you a chance to look more closely at what's happening in your life and why. I listen without judgment and ask questions that help you notice the beliefs, patterns, and influences shaping your choices. Together, we explore what's getting in the way, what you want to change, and what you can do differently.

Get clear

Getting clear is about more than setting a goal. We look at where you are now, what you want to change, and what matters to you. From there, you can make choices that are more aligned with your values and the life you want.

Explore what repeats and get unstuck

Many of us repeat patterns without fully noticing them. Part of my role is to help you get unstuck from old patterns that may be keeping you from what you want and from living up to your potential. We look at where these patterns show up in your life, then explore a different way forward.

Turn insight into action

Understanding yourself becomes useful when it changes something in your everyday life. We turn what you discover into realistic next steps that fit your circumstances and help you build momentum over time.
