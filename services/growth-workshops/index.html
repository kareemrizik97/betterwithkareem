For organisations

Growth workshops for teams

Custom workshops for organisations that want to support employee wellbeing while strengthening communication and creating more thoughtful ways of working together.

Built around your team

I have spent the past five years working as a trainer in the NGO sector, where I have trained more than 2,000 people. That experience has shown me that the most useful workshops begin with a conversation about what your team is navigating. From there, each workshop is shaped by the people in the room and the kind of working culture you want to build.

A shared space

Practical growth, made relevant to your team.

These interactive sessions give teams room to step out of their day to day work and think more intentionally about how they work together. They draw on real workplace experiences and centre the conversations that matter. The intention is for the work to remain useful long after people return to their day.

Topics that matter

Workshops can focus on emotional intelligence, social skills, employee wellbeing, or communication. Each topic is approached with the aim of creating a healthier working environment where people can contribute with greater awareness.

Made to be practical

Participants leave with ideas and tools they can use beyond the workshop. The work may help them communicate more effectively or support wellbeing with more intention. The aim is not to overwhelm people with theory. It is to give them something they can genuinely use.

Tailored to your context

A workshop can be a focused session around one topic or a more spacious conversation across several areas. Together, we shape the workshop around your team’s goals and the realities they are facing. We will decide what format works best for the time you have available.
