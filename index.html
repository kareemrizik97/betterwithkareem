Meet Kareem

Coaching that leaves room for the person you already are.

I’m Kareem Rizik, a Certified Life Coach with a deep interest in psychology, human behavior, and personal growth.

What interests me most is understanding why we do what we do. Why we get stuck, why we repeat certain patterns, and why sometimes we know exactly what we want but still struggle to move toward it.

I believe we all have the capacity to grow and move toward the life we want. Sometimes, though, we get stuck along the way. Limiting beliefs, old patterns, fear, or the way we’ve learned to see ourselves can hold us back, even when a part of us knows we want something different.

That’s a big part of how I approach coaching. I combine coaching with what I continue to learn from psychology to help you understand yourself better, uncover what may be keeping you stuck, and get clearer on where you genuinely want to go.

I also believe that growth doesn’t come from constantly fighting or rejecting parts of yourself. There’s value in learning to accept yourself as you are, while also being honest about what you want to change. The more we understand ourselves and live in a way that feels aligned with who we really are, the easier it becomes to move forward.

I’m not here to tell you how to live your life or give you all the answers. My role is to listen without judgment, ask the right questions, challenge you when needed, help you work through the beliefs and patterns standing between you and where you want to be, and turn that understanding into practical action steps that move you forward.

For me, coaching isn’t about becoming a completely different person. It’s about understanding yourself more deeply, getting unstuck, and becoming more of the person you genuinely want to be.

Questions

Before we begin.

Want to ask something that is not covered here? You are welcome to get in touch or start with a discovery conversation.

Who is coaching for?

Coaching can be useful for people navigating change, feeling stuck, working through a decision, or wanting to build a life with more intention. The first conversation is a good place to explore whether it fits what you need.

What happens after I book?

You’ll receive a confirmation email with the appointment details and next steps. If a paid session applies, payment is completed securely as part of the booking process.

Are sessions online or in person?

Session format and availability are shown when you book. Details can also be confirmed in your initial conversation.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is not therapy, medical care, or crisis support. It is a collaborative, future-oriented space for reflection, choices, and practical movement. If you need mental-health or emergency support, please contact an appropriately qualified local professional or emergency service.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

The booking confirmation includes the current rescheduling and cancellation process. Please review the policy shown during booking before confirming your appointment.
