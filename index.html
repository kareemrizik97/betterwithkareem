Questions

Before we begin.

Want to ask something that isn’t covered here? You’re welcome to get in touch or start with a discovery conversation.

Who is coaching for?

Coaching can be useful for people navigating change, feeling stuck, working through a decision, or wanting to build a life with more intention. The first conversation is a good place to explore whether this approach fits what you need.

What happens after I book?

You’ll receive a confirmation email with the appointment details and next steps. If a paid session applies, payment is completed securely as part of the booking process.

Are sessions online or in person?

Session format and availability are shown when you book. Details can also be confirmed in your initial conversation.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is not therapy, medical care, or crisis support. It is a collaborative, future-oriented space for reflection, choices, and practical movement. If you need mental-health or emergency support, please contact an appropriately qualified local professional or emergency service.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

The booking confirmation includes the current rescheduling and cancellation process. Please review the policy shown during booking before confirming your appointment.
