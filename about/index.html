About me

Meet Kareem.

A little about the person and approach behind BetterwithKareem.

My approach

Coaching that leaves room for the person you already are.

I’m Kareem Rizik, a Certified Life Coach with a deep interest in psychology and human behavior. Personal growth is at the heart of my work.

What interests me most is understanding why we do what we do. I am especially curious about the patterns that keep us stuck, even when we know what we want.

I believe we all have the capacity to grow and move toward the life we want. Sometimes the beliefs and patterns we carry can make that movement feel difficult.

That shapes how I approach coaching. I bring together coaching and what I continue to learn from psychology. My aim is to help you understand yourself better and see what may be keeping you stuck. From there, you can get clearer on the direction you genuinely want to take.

I also believe that growth does not come from constantly fighting or rejecting parts of yourself. It comes from accepting yourself as you are while being honest about what you want to change. As we understand ourselves and live in a way that feels aligned with who we are, moving forward becomes easier.

I’m not here to tell you how to live your life or give you all the answers. My role is to listen without judgment and ask the questions that help you see more clearly. When it is useful, I will challenge you with care. Together, we can work through what is standing in your way and turn understanding into practical steps.

For me, coaching is not about becoming a completely different person. It is about understanding yourself more deeply so you can get unstuck and become more of the person you genuinely want to be.
