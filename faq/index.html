FAQ

What would you like to know?

Want to ask something that is not covered here? You are welcome to get in touch or start with a discovery conversation.

Who is coaching for?

Coaching can be helpful when you feel stuck, keep repeating the same patterns, or know you want something to change but don’t know how to move forward. It’s also for people who simply want to understand themselves better, get clearer on what they want, and start taking action toward it.

What happens after I book?

You’ll receive a confirmation email with the appointment details and next steps. If a paid session applies, payment is completed securely as part of the booking process.

Are sessions online or in person?

All coaching sessions are held online. Growth workshops can be offered online or in person, depending on your team’s needs.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is not a substitute for therapy, medical care, or crisis support. It is a collaborative space for reflection and practical movement. If you need mental health or emergency support, please contact an appropriately qualified local professional or emergency service.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

The booking confirmation includes the current rescheduling and cancellation process. Please review the policy shown during booking before confirming your appointment.
